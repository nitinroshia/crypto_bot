### New-phase order (embed verbatim as Section 7)

*Mathematician, 2026-09-20.* Costs: fee 0.10% per side plus slippage 0.02% per side (base round trip 0.24%); stress 0.12% plus 0.03% per side (0.30%); taker at the next bar's open; promotions never modeled.

**1. Materiality and classification**
- M = 0.35% gross per round trip, measured as the excess over the unconditional mean forward return at the same horizon, so drift alone cannot pass. It applies to event-type primary cells (Task 3).
- Pass = one-sided p < 0.05 and excess ≥ M. Dead = one-sided 95% upper bound below M. Otherwise inconclusive.
- Forward success = one-sided p < 0.05 and an estimate at least half the discovery estimate, and also ≥ M for event cells.
- Report MDE from the standard error.

**5. Specs**

*S2 `funding_extreme`* (family 2; ETHUSDT perpetual funding, ETH/USDT spot prices)
- Predictor: funding rate at each settlement s; rank = its percentile within the previous 270 settlements (current excluded).
- Target: ETH/USDT spot log return from the open of the 1h bar starting at s+1h to +24h and +72h.
- Test: OLS slope of forward return on rank, all settlements, Newey-West HAC (3 lags for 24h, 9 for 72h). Direction negative. Primary: 72h.
- Exploratory, descriptive only: mean forward return and excess in rank ≤ 0.10 and ≥ 0.90.
- Plausible if real: correlation −0.03 to −0.08; above 0.2 means a bug.

*S1 `trend_slope`* (ETH/USDT 1d; family 10)
- Predictor at the close of day t: x = ln(C_t / C_{t−L}) / σ30, where σ30 = std of the last 30 daily log returns ending at t; L ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80, 160}.
- Target: ln(C_{t+h} / C_t), h ∈ {1, 5}; all daily origins; Newey-West HAC (5 lags for h = 1, 10 for h = 5). Direction positive.
- Primary: L = 40, h = 5. The other nine cells are exploratory (Holm within the 10).
- Gut-check: x uses closes ≤ t; y uses closes > t.
