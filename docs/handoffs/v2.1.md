## 6. Decisions in force (work order 1.2, plus items of work order 1.1 / answers 01-02 not superseded)

**Cost model.** Fee 0.10% per side plus slippage 0.02% per side (base, 0.24% round trip); stress 0.12% plus 0.03% per side (0.30%); taker at the next bar's open; promotions never modeled.

**Materiality and classification.** M = 0.35% gross per round trip for event-type primary cells (Task 3), measured as the excess over the unconditional mean forward return at the same horizon. Pass = one-sided p < 0.05 and excess at least M; Dead = one-sided 95% upper bound below M; otherwise inconclusive. Forward success = one-sided p < 0.05 and an estimate at least half the discovery estimate, and also at least M for event cells. Report the minimum detectable effect from the standard error.

**Sample design.** ETH/USDT is the research pair. Discovery and walk-forward use data up to and including 2025-08-31, enforced by an `--end-date` guard; later data is a locked holdout readable only by the forward-test command, once per candidate, Holm across candidates that reach it (registry). Walk-forward on ETH/USDT: fit 365 / validate 90 days; ETH/FDUSD keeps its post-break 90/30 rules, reference only. Replace the by-month table with a by-year table plus leave-one-year-out; if dropping any single year cuts the estimate by more than half or flips its sign, the candidate does not advance without the mathematician's review.

**Pass rules (unchanged unless the mathematician says otherwise).**
- Holm is the gate within each pre-registered family (Bonferroni for reference); cumulative count reported; every cell tagged `pre-registered` or `exploratory`; correlation reported beside beta.
- Lag selection in a fit window: smallest p (largest |HAC t|), no "significant" pre-filter, not |beta| or |correlation|. (The code still selects the largest |beta| among significant lags; this is queue item 1 of section 7.)
- Walk-forward is a stability check, not independent evidence. Validate at the fit-selected lag: (a) same sign in every window; (b) one-sided Stouffer of validate z (z = orientation sign x HAC t, equal weights), p < 0.05, Holm across candidates carried forward; (c) no split significantly opposite (two-sided p < 0.05, opposite sign). Report the selected lag per split; jumping lags are a warning.
- Orientation sign: the pre-registered direction for pre-registered cells (S1 positive, S2 negative, no lag selected); each split's fit-window sign for two-sided cells and lag scans (Task 3).
- Freeze: a manifest timestamp fixing the exact cell, direction, discovery estimate and dispersion, and the code commit; one forward test on the holdout, then consumed; failure means dead, no retuning.
- Resampling: traded-minutes rule (adopted; zero mismatches on the real ETH/FDUSD data).

**Confirmed Task 3 and Task 4 interpretations (kept).** Task 3: 13 bars between kept events (not 12). Task 4: hour-of-day (24 levels) instead of half-hour; the volume ratio compares the same 60-minute clock window against its median over the prior 14 days; the 5-day range uses closed daily bars only. Task 3 changes per work order 1.2: the pair is ETH/USDT under the cutoff above; report the raw mean and the excess over the unconditional mean; the three-way rule with M = 0.35% replaces the earlier triage.

**Carried over from answer 02, not restated in work order 1.2 (listed as a doubt in the close-out report):** the forward-test minimum sample (at least 45 days, at least 100 events or trades, standard error no larger than half the claimed effect).

**5. Specs**

*S2 `funding_extreme`* (family 2; ETHUSDT perpetual funding, ETH/USDT spot prices)
- Predictor: funding rate at each settlement s; rank = its percentile within the previous 270 settlements (current excluded).
- Target: ETH/USDT spot log return from the open of the 1h bar starting at s+1h to +24h and +72h.
- Test: OLS slope of forward return on rank, all settlements, Newey-West HAC (3 lags for 24h, 9 for 72h). Direction negative. Primary: 72h.

*S1 `trend_slope`* (ETH/USDT 1d; family 10)
- Predictor at the close of day t: x = ln(C_t / C_{t−L}) / σ30, where σ30 = std of the last 30 daily log returns ending at t; L ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80, 160}.
- Target: ln(C_{t+h} / C_t), h ∈ {1, 5}; all daily origins; Newey-West HAC (5 lags for h = 1, 10 for h = 5). Direction positive.
- Primary: L = 40, h = 5. The other nine cells are exploratory (Holm within the 10).
- Gut-check: x uses closes ≤ t; y uses closes > t.

## 9. Lessons from the process

- Several uploads were stale copies; this caused two rounds of confusion (the 1m extension had in fact worked at 00:24 UTC on 2026-09-20; the first backup proves it). Always check a file's internal evidence (row counts, new keys) before concluding a step failed.
- `fetch_klines.py` extends backwards by re-fetching when the existing file starts later than the job start, then merging; the freqtrade downloader did not backfill, hence `--export-freqtrade` (backup + identity checks, refuses on any mismatch).
- Mistakes fixed in shared tools: positional pairing across unequal date ranges (about 84% of 5m bars were mis-paired against the original 1m); walk-forward re-scanned all lags in validate; fit/validate boundary bar in both windows; column `lag_minutes_after_close` actually counted fine bars.
