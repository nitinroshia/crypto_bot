## Doubts about the embedded order
1. **Forward-test minimum sample.** Answer 02 required at least 45 days, at least 100 events or trades, and a standard error no larger than half the claimed effect. Work order 1.2 restates the success rule without them. With a fixed locked holdout they may be moot. I kept them in the handoff as unconfirmed.
2. **Materiality for the regression cells.** M = 0.35% applies to event-type primary cells (Task 3). S1 and S2 are slope cells with no stated economic threshold, so their "pass" reads as discovery Holm plus walk-forward plus forward p < 0.05 and half the estimate. Please confirm that is intended.
3. **S2 target endpoints.** "From the open of the 1h bar starting at s+1h to +24h and +72h" does not say whether the end is the open of the bar starting at s+25h and s+73h, or a close. The HAC lags (3 and 9) assume 8h settlement spacing; if the interval changes and is normalized to 8h, horizons should be counted in settlements, not clock hours. I will need the intended endpoints stated.
4. **Cutoff and forward targets.** Targets that start before 2025-08-31 but extend past it would use holdout data. The `--end-date` guard needs a rule (truncate the target, or drop the origin). Inclusive of which final bar is also unstated.
5. **Partial years.** The by-year table and leave-one-year-out include 2017 (starts 2017-08-17) and 2025 (ends 2025-08-31) as partial years. Whether to treat them as years, drop them, or merge them is not stated.
6. **Task 4 pair and sample.** Task 4 (kept, only if a candidate passes) does not say ETH/USDT or ETH/FDUSD.
