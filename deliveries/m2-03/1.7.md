## 1. Definition (families `Task3-ETHUSDT` and `Task3-BTCUSDT`; same code; 48 cells per pair)

1. **Bars and masking.** Timeframes 5m and 15m on the spot USDT pair. Both come from the 5m file: the 15m bars are derived from the
   5m bars by the project's validated resampling rule for BOTH pairs (a 15m bar with any of its three 5m bars missing is missing; if
   ETH has a native 15m file, check that it equals the derived series on every complete bar, report the mismatches, expect 0, and use
   the derived series). A bar is present if it exists with open, high, low and close; missing bars are masked, never filled. Bars are
   left-labelled; width w = 5 or 15 minutes. A return exists only if bar j and bar j-1 are present and consecutive:
   r_j = ln(C_j / C_(j-1)); otherwise it is masked. The loader truncates the discovery frame at the cutoff (last bars: 5m 23:55,
   15m 23:45 on 2025-08-31).
2. **z and events.** Window W = 288 returns for 5m (24h) and 96 for 15m. z_j = r_j / sd_j, sd_j = sample standard deviation (ddof = 1)
   of r_(j-W) .. r_(j-1). Bar j is eligible for the event clock only if r_j and all W of those returns are unmasked and sd_j > 0
   (count the others). An event bar has |z_j| >= 3: down if z_j <= -3, up if z_j >= +3. The decision is taken at the close of bar j;
   the entry is the open of bar j+1.
3. **De-clustering.** One shared clock per timeframe and pair: scan eligible event bars in time order, keep the first; after keeping
   bar j ignore every event bar before j+13; the next kept event is the first event bar at or after j+13. Both sides share the clock.
   The clock uses only information available at the event bar: a kept event whose target window later proves incomplete (item 4)
   stays on the clock and is dropped from the statistics and counted. (Labelled robustness variant: one clock per side.)
4. **Targets and baseline.** For a kept event at bar j: R_k = ln(C_(j+k) / O_(j+1)), k = 1..12, the log return from the open of bar
   j+1 to the close of bar j+k. An event with any of the bars j+1 .. j+12 missing, or whose bar j+12 closes after the cutoff, is excluded
   from all k and counted (one common event set). mu_k = the mean of R_k over ALL origins of that timeframe in the discovery
   sample, event bars included; an origin is any bar j with a valid z window, bars j+1 .. j+12 present and bar j+12 closing at or before the
   cutoff. mu_k is a constant. Excess e = R_k - mu_k. (Labelled robustness variant: mu_k from the origins with |z| < 3 only.)
5. **Cells and test.** timeframe {5m, 15m} x k = 1..12 x side {down, up} = 48 per pair. Per cell report n, mean raw R_k, mean excess,
   SE, t = mean excess / SE, MDE = 2.486 x SE. SE is the day-clustered SE of the mean: clusters are the UTC calendar days of the entry
   bar, SE = sqrt( sum over clusters c of ( sum over events i in c of (e_i - mean) )^2 ) / n. Also report the naive SE = sd / sqrt(n) and its
   t as a labelled column. P-values from the normal distribution. The two PRIMARY cells are (5m, k = 6, down) and (5m, k = 6, up),
   both pre-registered one-sided POSITIVE (buy after a down-shock is a reversal bet, buy after an up-shock a continuation bet; spot
   is long-only either way): p = 1 - Phi(t). The other 46 cells have no pre-registered direction: two-sided p, descriptive; they cannot
   pass any gate and may only nominate a NEW pre-registered family. Holm and Bonferroni over the 48 cells of the pair (the primary
   cells' one-sided p and the other cells' two-sided p enter the same Holm); report the family size 48 and the adjusted values.
6. **Classification** of the two primary cells, M = 0.35% = 0.0035 (log units) on the excess over mu_6: PASS = Holm-adjusted one-sided
   p < 0.05 and mean excess >= M; DEAD = the one-sided 95% upper bound (mean excess + 1.645 x SE) < M and MDE <= M; otherwise
   INCONCLUSIVE.
7. **Dose-response check** (a discard rule, not a test; per side, 5m, k = 6): repeat items 2-4 with threshold |z| >= 4 and its own fresh
   shared clock (a new event set, not a subset of the |z| >= 3 kept events). Discard the |z| >= 3 result if the |z| >= 4 mean excess
   is smaller AND the |z| >= 4 sample has at least 30 events; with fewer than 30 events mark "not evaluable" and flag it for M2.
   Report both estimates with their counts.
8. **Sanity targets** (if the 5m files start at 2017-08-17 04:00): 845,808 5m bars in a gap-free discovery frame; first eligible 5m
   origin 2017-08-18 04:05 (15m: 04:15); last eligible origin 22:55 on 2025-08-31 (15m: 20:45). Report the counts of bars, eligible
   origins and the reasons for non-eligibility (z window, target window, cutoff), event bars before and after de-clustering, kept
   events per side and the number dropped for incomplete windows, for 5m and 15m, both pairs, for |z| >= 3 and |z| >= 4. Explain any
   kept count below 500 or above 20,000 per side at 5m before the live run, and any asymmetry above a factor of 1.5 between sides.

## 2. Stability, by-year, robustness, trade lists, registry

- **Stability** of each primary cell separately (replaces "standard rolling walk-forward" for Task 3: both directions are
  pre-registered, so there is nothing to fit). Reuse only the block tiling: B' full blocks of 90 CALENDAR days tiled from t0 = the entry
  time (open of bar j0+1) of the first eligible origin j0 of the 5m set; block b holds the kept events of the cell's side whose entry
  time falls in [t0 + 90b days, t0 + 90(b+1) days); only blocks whose whole window lies inside [t0, entry time of the last eligible
  origin] are scored (expect 32 full blocks and a partial block of about 55 days, both pairs). For each full block with at least 30
  events: t_b = mean(e) / (sd(e) / sqrt(n_b)) over the block's k = 6 excesses; blocks with fewer than 30 events are listed and
  excluded (count them). T = sum t_b / sqrt(B'), B' = blocks used. p_stab = 1 - Phi(T) (one-sided, positive). Pass: p_stab < 0.05,
  then Holm across candidates per pair (R1), each primary cell being its own entry. Store T, p_stab, B', and the per-block n_b and
  t_b. Why no simulated null: events are non-overlapping and decided from past information only, so with no predictability the block
  t's are close to independent standard normals; the reference gives false-pass rates of 3.0-5.5% (section 4).
- **By-year and leave-one-year-out** for both primary cells: per calendar year n, mean excess, SE, t; the leave-one-year-out pooled
  mean excess; the non-stationarity rule of NOTES section 6 (year-driven if dropping any single year cuts the estimate by more than
  half or flips its sign; years under 120 days shown, not judged: with files starting 2017-08-18 no year is that short, 2017 has 136
  days).
- **Robustness columns** (descriptive, labelled, primary cells only): (a) one clock per side; (b) mu_6 from the |z| < 3 origins only;
  (c) entry one bar late: ln(C_(j+1+6) / O_(j+2)) against its own baseline mean over all origins; (d) naive SE and t; (e) the |z| >= 4
  numbers of item 7; (f) mean raw return minus the round-trip cost at base (0.24%) and stress (0.30%), as information (the
  classification uses the excess against M); (g) kept events by UTC hour of the event bar, 24 counts per side.
- **Trade lists** for the later after-tax layer (owner's roadmap): for each pair and primary cell save the kept events as a CSV
  committed with the report: pair, cell, event_bar_time_utc, entry_time_utc (open of bar j+1), exit_time_utc (close of bar j+6),
  entry_price (O_(j+1)), exit_price (C_(j+6)), gross_log_return, excess. About 5,000 rows each. Everything in the report is pre-tax:
  label it so.
- **Registry** (family `Task3-<PAIR>`, live only after the dry run and the owner's explicit flag): (1) discovery: all 48 cells with the
  two primary cell ids `5m_k6_down` and `5m_k6_up`, each with raw one-sided p, Holm, classification; (2) stability: per primary
  cell T, p_stab, B'. No economic-gate record: M is the economic criterion of event cells. Records are written whatever the outcome
  (as for S1 and S2). Each primary cell is a separate entry in every across-candidate family at the stability step; report raw and
  adjusted values. As in item 6 of `answer-s2-rulings.md`, S1's and S2's across-candidate Holm rows legitimately change once these records exist.

## 6. Gut-checks (mandatory)

1. No look-ahead: the volatility estimate excludes the event bar's own return; the decision is at the close of bar j and the entry
   at the open of bar j+1; the clock never depends on whether later bars exist (mistake 19).
2. Shared latent state: events cluster in high-volatility spells and at certain hours. The excess is measured against one global
   constant mu_k, the drift at 30 minutes is negligible, and the SE is clustered by day. Never quote a raw mean as an effect: a raw
   mean after down-shocks can be positive simply because the market drifted up (mistake 11).
3. Microstructure: a reversal in the first bar after a shock can be bid-ask bounce or a stale price (mistake 10). If the primary
   effect disappears with the entry one bar late (robustness column c), it is not tradable by a retail taker; say so.
4. Calibration: this order's tests were null-calibrated and power-checked before being pre-registered (mistakes 14, 15, 18). If your
   null rows are outside the bands, stop and report as a file; do not change the statistic.
5. Bug signals: no kept events; sides differing by more than a factor of 1.5; any |excess| above 1% at 60 minutes; an MDE above M;
   a p-value of exactly 0 or 1 in many cells.
